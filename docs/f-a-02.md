# f-a-02 — Attempt and Decision Extraction

Lane: **Person 1 — Capture (sources to Atlas)** · Covers: R2–R5, R8

## Goal

Turn labeled messages into structured attempts and decisions with typed blockers and evidence.

## User-visible behavior

A message that reports a failure becomes an attempt with goal, approach, outcome, blockers, evidence, conditions, and hours. A decision message becomes a decision.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- OpenAI structured extraction with zod schemas from `lib/types.ts`.
- Embeddings written on attempts and decisions.
- Supersede links when a decision replaces an earlier one.

Out of scope:

- Merging (`f-a-03`).

## Acceptance criteria

- Every blocker has one of the seven R3 types.
- Superseded decisions are marked `superseded` with `supersededBy`.

## Verification steps

1. Run the extraction schema tests.
2. Extract the Postgres LIKE result and verify blockers, evidence, and 9 hours.
3. Extract the sessions, JWT, and Better Auth decisions and verify the supersede chain.
