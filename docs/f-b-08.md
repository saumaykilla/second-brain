# f-b-08 — Harness Lab and Impact

Lane: **Person 2 — Recall (Atlas to answers and UI)** · Covers: R26, R27

## Goal

Show how the harness improved and what it saved.

## User-visible behavior

Harness Lab shows versions, scores, and diffs, and can run reflection. Impact shows warnings sent, hours saved, and the precision trend.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- `/lab` following `design/07-harness-lab.png`.
- `/impact` following `design/08-impact.png`.

Out of scope:

- Nightly schedule.

## Acceptance criteria

- Hours saved equals the sum of hours for helpful warnings.
- Run reflection updates the list after it finishes.

## Verification steps

1. Open Lab and verify two versions and a diff.
2. Run reflection from the button.
3. Open Impact and check the hours-saved sum.
