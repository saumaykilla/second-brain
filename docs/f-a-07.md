# f-a-07 — Revisitable Conditions

Lane: **Person 1 — Capture (sources to Atlas)** · Covers: R18, R33

## Goal

Reopen a dead end when a later decision meets its conditions.

## User-visible behavior

When a new decision satisfies a dead end's conditions, ProjectBrain marks that attempt revisitable and says which blocker may no longer apply.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- Real `checkConditions` contract: candidate attempts by vector search, strong-model check, status change, and an `unblocks` edge.

Out of scope:

- Timeline color (`f-b-04`).

## Acceptance criteria

- The App Runner decision flips the WebSockets attempt to `revisitable`.
- An unrelated decision flips nothing.
- Replaying the decision causes one transition.

## Verification steps

1. Capture the App Runner decision.
2. Verify the WebSockets status and explanation.
3. Verify an unrelated decision changes nothing.
4. Verify the `unblocks` edge.
5. Replay and verify one transition.
