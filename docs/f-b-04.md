# f-b-04 — Timeline and Dead-End Detail

Lane: **Person 2 — Recall (Atlas to answers and UI)** · Covers: R23, R24

## Goal

Show the project's memory over time and the full story of each dead end.

## User-visible behavior

The Orbit timeline shows six weeks of decisions and attempts. Failed attempts are red, revisitable ones amber, and a dead end opens to goal, approach, blockers, evidence, conditions, hours, and the alternative.

In scope:

- `/attempts/[id]` following `design/02-dead-end-detail.png`.

Out of scope:

- Four dead ends and the auth chain appear in order.
- Revisitable attempts are amber.
