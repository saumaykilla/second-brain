# f-b-05 — Slack Proactive Warning

Lane: **Person 2 — Recall (Atlas to answers and UI)** · Covers: R13, R15, R29

## Goal

Reply in the Slack thread when an intent matches a dead end.

## User-visible behavior

When someone says in Slack they are about to repeat a failed approach, ProjectBrain replies in that thread with the dead end, evidence, alternative, and hours saved.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- Warning handler called by `f-a-06` for intent messages.
- Threaded reply following `design/09-slack-warning.png`.
- `warnings` record.
- Helpful and Not relevant buttons stored as feedback.

Out of scope:

- Receiving events (`f-a-06`).

## Acceptance criteria

- The socket.io fixture produces one threaded warning.
- A non-match posts nothing.
- Button clicks are stored.

## Verification steps

1. Run the handler against the socket.io fixture with a mocked Slack client.
2. Verify a non-match posts nothing.
3. Verify the warning record.
4. Click Not relevant and verify feedback.

## Dependencies

- `f-b-01` Dead-End Check
- `f-a-06` Slack Event Capture
