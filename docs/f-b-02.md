# f-b-02 — Check an Idea

Lane: **Person 2 — Recall (Atlas to answers and UI)** · Covers: R15, R25

## Goal

Let a member paste a plan and see matching dead ends before building.

## User-visible behavior

On `/check`, a pasted plan shows similar dead ends with confidence, evidence, alternative, and hours, or an explicit no-match state.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- `/check` page following `design/04-check-match.png` and `design/05-check-none.png`.
- Mark not relevant, stored as feedback.

Out of scope:

- Slack.

## Acceptance criteria

- The socket.io plan shows the WebSockets card at its confidence.
- A new idea shows the no-match state.

## Verification steps

1. Paste the socket.io plan in the browser.
2. Paste a new idea and see no match.
3. Mark a result not relevant and read the feedback back.
4. Check loading and error states.

- `f-sh-05` App Shell and Design Tokens
