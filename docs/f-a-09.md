# f-a-09 — AWS Hosting and Worker

Lane: **Person 1 — Capture (sources to Atlas)** · Covers: R36

## Goal

Run the app on AWS and move capture work out of the web request.

## User-visible behavior

ProjectBrain runs on AWS, and captures are processed by a background worker.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- Infrastructure definition for the app and worker.
- A queue from capture to the worker.
- A schedule hook that `f-b-07` uses for nightly reflection.

Out of scope:

- Nightly reflection logic (owned by `f-b-07`).

## Acceptance criteria

- The worker processes a capture outside the request.
- The infra definition validates without deploying production.

## Verification steps

1. Validate the infrastructure definition.
2. Run the worker entrypoint on a fixture capture job.
3. Invoke the schedule hook the way the schedule would.
