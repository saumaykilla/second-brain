# f-a-08 — Evidence Storage

Lane: **Person 1 — Capture (sources to Atlas)** · Covers: R4, R36

## Goal

Store evidence files in S3 and open them from a dead end.

## User-visible behavior

Evidence files such as logs and benchmarks can be opened from a dead-end record.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- S3 upload during capture.
- Signed, short-lived read URLs.
- `evidence[].url` on attempts.

Out of scope:

- Public buckets.

## Acceptance criteria

- Evidence opens from the detail screen.
- URLs expire.

## Verification steps

1. Upload a fixture log and open it from the WebSockets attempt.
2. Confirm the URL expires.
