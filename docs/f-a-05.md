# f-a-05 — Orbit Seed Through the Pipeline

Lane: **Person 1 — Capture (sources to Atlas)** · Covers: R30–R33

## Goal

Produce Orbit's six-week history by running about 150 seed messages through the real pipeline.

## User-visible behavior

The Orbit project shows six weeks of history, including four dead ends, a superseded auth chain, and a later decision that can reopen the WebSockets dead end.

## Scope / out of scope

In scope:

- `seed/orbit-messages.json` (about 150 messages over six weeks).
- `pnpm seed:orbit` that calls `ingestMessage` for each one.

Out of scope:

- Inserting finished records directly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Four attempts with the R31 outcomes, hours, and alternatives.
- The R32 supersede chain.
- Records come from extraction, not inserts.

## Verification steps

1. Run the seed on an empty database.
2. Confirm the four dead ends and their hours.
3. Confirm the supersede chain.
4. Diff seeded records against `fixtures/orbit.json`.
